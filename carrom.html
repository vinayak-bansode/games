How To Play

  • 1 Use the Slider below the board to position your striker along your baseline.
  • 2 Click and drag backward from the striker to set aiming angle and shot power.
  • 3 Release mouse/touch to fire. Trajectory dots predict shot paths!
  • 4 Queen Rule: Pocketing the Red Queen awards 3 points, but you must cover it by pocketing one of your pieces on the same or next turn.
Point System: White Pucks = 2 pts | Black Pucks = 1 pt
Queen = 3 pts (Must cover) | Foul = -1 pt
